# V6.2 - Connected roads, station access, North Meadows, jet skis, island walkways

**Roads and bridges**

- The three river bridges now sit 4 m above the water. They end 4 m past each bank, so their approach ramps are short and gentle (grade 8.5% or less).
- Each approach road follows the bridge deck height exactly, and the terrain is graded to the road.
- Heron Bridge Road crosses Lake Road at a clean junction south of Heron Lake Station. It then runs over the west bridge to the north shore.
- Quarry Lane continues north from Ridgeview Crescent through North Meadows and over the centre bridge. Owawa Road crosses the east bridge into downtown with even shoulders.
- Where each bridge road meets a north-shore street, the curb and sidewalk open for it.

**Stations**

- Every station's ground access is defined once, in `STATION_ACCESS`: the lift tower beside the platform, the entrance pavilion, a level forecourt and a footpath to the street.
- Rendering, colliders, the E prompt, train exits, the map, trees and grass all read `STATION_ACCESS`, so they stay in step.
- Owawa Quay moved 35 m along the line, onto dry ground east of Owawa Road. Its doors now face the road across a forecourt, with a short path to the shoulder and a walk north along the road to the bridge. Previously its lift and doors stood in the bridge approach.
- Heron Lake Station's forecourt joins Heron Bridge Road by a footpath.
- Ridge West's lift moved off the West Link boulevard.
- The other stations gained paths:
  - the Airport to the Airport Drive sidewalk;
  - the University to the campus path;
  - Ridge Centre and South Commons through walkways between lots to the nearest street.
- Each footpath has a roadside sign: "STATION NAME STATION · EXPRESS RAIL · LIFT TO PLATFORM".
- Platforms and canopies follow the track. Leaving a train puts you in front of the station doors, facing out.
- Rail support columns move along the track to clear every carriageway, shoulder, sidewalk, forecourt, footpath, building and lot. Each platform has a column near it.

**North Meadows (north of the panel factory)**

- A connected grid of streets:
  - Quarry Lane, Ridgeview Crescent and Mill Road;
  - Fieldstone Avenue, Clover Crescent and Meadow Way;
  - Sorrel Lane and Meadow Court.
- Housing, all set back from the street with driveways:
  - 36′ detached lots;
  - freehold town rows, each terrace with one palette;
  - some homes still under construction.
- East of Mill Road:
  - Meadows Market (grocer and cafe);
  - Meadows Medical and offices;
  - Meadowview Condos;
  - Meadows Business Centre;
  - Riverwalk Condos;
  - two parking courts entered from Mill Road.
- Nothing overlaps: not lots, streets, buildings or parking.

**Watercraft**

- Each harbour (Heron Lake Marina, Heron Gardens Harbour, Owawa River Quay) has one motorboat and one jet ski. Each harbour's jet ski has its own colour.
- The jet ski is light and quick:
  - top speed about 76 km/h;
  - sharper steering than the motorboat;
  - a short coast;
  - little reverse.
- To ride: walk onto the pier and press F. Steer with W/S and A/D. Press F beside a pier or a dry shore to get off. In open water, F does nothing.
- The ambient river ships are gone. Townspeople now ride jet skis in loops on five open reaches of the river, clear of the bridges, piers and the island.

**Heron Gardens Island**

- New walkways:
  - a perimeter loop behind the homes and along the shop fronts;
  - a central promenade past Elias's shack;
  - a cross path between the buildings;
  - a path to every home and shop door;
  - paths down the beach onto the harbour pier head and to Elias's fishing jetty.
- Walkways follow the ground and the pier decks, avoid buildings and trees, appear on the map and have no grass on them. The island stays detached: no road or bridge reaches it.
