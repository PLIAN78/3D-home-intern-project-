# Caivan: Build It! — V6 River City

V6 expands the existing 2 km world with the Owawa River, two Heron Lake islands, a north-shore city, university, suburbs, airport and express rail.

- Heron Gardens: Caivan showroom, general store, sushi shop, homes and a harbour. Elias keeps the smaller island.
- The north shore mixes heritage buildings, retail, mid-rises, offices and towers. City pedestrian spawning is nine per loop against the Ridge's three; city car counts remain four and three on the original two traffic loops.
- Riverbank Financial and Northshore Residences have hollow lobbies and furnished upper suites. Press E at their entrances for the elevator. The appraisal visit pays $900; a $14,000 condo pays $325 rent every two minutes of play.
- University departments teach engineering, science, business and history. First correct answers pay $180. Principal Chen recruits three subject-qualified co-ops; they follow you or work at the Caivan office for recurring pay.
- Seven express stations connect Ridge West, the airport, Heron Lake, Owawa Quay, Ridge Centre, the university and South Commons. Six trains run continuously, stop ten seconds and accelerate/brake at 4.5 m/s², reaching 250 km/h on the longest leg. Press E at a ground station sign to board; press E on a stopped train to leave.
- Over 200 new parcels mix construction, empty land and detached homes; low-rise condo buildings provide another housing type. The airport has a terminal, tower, runway and parked aircraft.
- More woodland and passive deer, foxes and rabbits. River vessels are ambient traffic that enter and leave the map; aircraft are scenery.
- V6 lessons, hired co-ops and condo ownership persist in this browser. Existing main-story progress retains the game's existing behavior.

Run: npm ci --ignore-scripts, npm run game. Build: npm run game:build.
Validation: npm test -- tests/game.test.ts tests/sim.test.ts tests/v5.test.ts tests/v6.test.ts tests/roadStrips.test.ts.
