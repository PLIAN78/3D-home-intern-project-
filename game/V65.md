# V6.5: police chases, a green riverbank, the island in open water, real-looking animals, and the south-west woods

**Police**

- **New look.** The cruisers have black doors with a POLICE decal, a blue pinstripe, a unit number, a push bar, grille lights, a spotlight, an antenna and a roof light bar. The bar's red and blue halves flash slowly on patrol and fast in a chase.
- **Chases.** Drive into a cruiser and it turns on its siren and follows you for 10 seconds. It closes in fast, brakes in time and then holds back a few metres, matching your speed. Hit it again during the chase and the 10 seconds start over. When the time runs out it gives up and drives back along its own route to where it left its beat.
- **Siren.** A two-tone wail plays while a cruiser is chasing you. It gets quieter with distance and goes silent beyond about 260 m.
- Toasts tell you when a chase starts ("It's on your tail") and when the police give up.

**The river**

- **Green north bank.** The paving north of the river now stops at the riverfront street's south sidewalk. Between the sidewalk and the water there is lawn, with two staggered rows of trees. The rows leave gaps at the bridges, the quay and the beach.
- **Riverside Beach.** A new beach on the north bank, across from Meadows Beach. It has sand, umbrellas, a lifeguard chair, a volleyball net and a sign.
- **Heron Gardens Island** has moved into the open water, halfway between the north and south shores, with about 135 m of water on each side. Everything on it moved with it: its streets, homes, harbour piers, Elias's jetty and the trees.
  - The tour ship now swings round the island's north side.
  - The jet-ski riders who used the reach west of the island were too close to Elias's jetty, so that reach has no riders now.

**Animals**

The [3DAnimals](https://github.com/3DAnimals/3DAnimals) repository contains research code for reconstructing animals from photos. It has no 3D models that could be used in the game. The animals are instead built on one hand-made rig that works the way those models do: a smooth body, a neck and a head, a tail, and four legs with two segments each.

- **Bodies** are shaped from a side profile, with a lighter belly. Legs taper from the thigh to a hoof or a paw. Each species has its own ears, tail, antlers or horns, and a beard or mane where it has one.
- **Gaits** follow speed. Horses, deer and goats walk, trot and gallop. Bears amble, foxes bound and rabbits hop.
- **Biscuit the horse** and **the goats** now use this rig.
- **Peanut the elephant** has a new body: a barrel that is higher at the shoulders and darker underneath, legs that bend at the knee, wrinkle folds on the legs and trunk, curved tusks, heavy eyelids and a tufted tail. Her trunk curls at the tip when she stands still.
- **Wildlife.** The old shapes are replaced with 41 animals:
  - deer herds, each with a stag, on five meadows;
  - three black bears at their dens, one with a cub;
  - eight foxes and fourteen rabbits in the woods.

  They wander near home and stop to graze or look around. Deer, foxes and rabbits run away when you get close. Bears don't. Every animal stays on dry land and leans with the slope.

**The south-west woods**

- **Mount Tamarack.** A 50 m mountain in the south-west corner of the forest. Its summit is bare, with a cairn, a flag and a sign. Boulders cover the summit and the slopes.
- **Creeks.** Two creeks run down from the mountain, one to the west and one past the campground to the south.
- **Rocks and bushes.** About 250 boulders lie through the woods, and the 110 larger ones are solid. There are about 400 bushes. Both keep clear of Cottage Road, the cottage yard and Peanut's clearing.
- **Pine Hollow Campground.** It is south of Rosa's cottage, at the end of a dirt trail from the cottage yard. The clearing is levelled, with:
  - a campfire with flickering flames, firelight, a stone ring and log benches;
  - four tents and two picnic tables;
  - a log pile, and a chopping stump with an axe;
  - a sign and the camp host.
- **Side job: Firewood for Pine Hollow ($180).**
  1. Talk to the camp host (E) to take the job.
  2. Chop down four of the trees marked with orange ribbons, just east of the campfire (E next to a tree). The side-job panel counts the trees as you chop them.
  3. Go back to the camp host to get paid.

  The trees grow back after you leave the area, so you can do the job again.
- **Map.** The map shows the mountain, the creeks, the trail and the clearing. It labels Mount Tamarack, Pine Hollow Campground and Riverside Beach.

**Tests**

`tests/v65.test.ts` covers:

- **Police:** the chase (it closes in and holds the gap, a second hit restarts the timer, it gives up and returns to its beat), a real crash setting off the alarm, and only cruisers giving chase.
- **The river:** the island's distance to each shore, the ship and jet skis staying clear of it, the green bank and its trees, and Riverside Beach.
- **The woods:** the mountain, the creeks running downhill, the level campground, rocks, bushes and trees staying off the campground and roads, the tents, and the firewood job from start to pay-out.
- **Wildlife:** where each animal lives, wandering on dry land, the cub keeping up with its mother, and which animals run away.

Some earlier tests changed because their rules changed on purpose:

- The hills limit now leaves out Mount Tamarack.
- The ship may leave the river channel to pass the island, but must stay in open water.
- The paving now stops at the riverfront sidewalk instead of in the river.
- The side-job test counts chopped trees for the firewood job.
