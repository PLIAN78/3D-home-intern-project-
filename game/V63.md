# V6.3: Owawa city, the walk-in mall, campus, sound, drifting and aircraft

**Bridges and water**

- The approach roads now run straight onto the bridge decks. Before, there was a slot at each end where the ground dipped toward the river. Now:
  - each deck end sits on an abutment fill, with a concrete face toward the river;
  - each end has a short level landing;
  - the ramps are at most 10%.
- The river and Heron Lake are one water surface. They used to be two overlapping surfaces, and they flickered where they met.
- The renderer uses a logarithmic depth buffer, which stops distant ground, paving and roads from flickering.
- Every shoreline now has a smooth bank strip that traces the exact waterline: an underwater shelf, wet sand, then grass.
- **Meadows Beach** is on the river's south bank below Meadow Way. It has sand, umbrellas, towels, a lifeguard chair and a volleyball net.

**Owawa**

- The whole north shore is now Owawa. The old grid in the top-right corner is **Downtown**.
- West of Downtown, a street grid runs from the riverfront to Bloor Street. Wellington, Rideau and Queen continue west from Downtown as Wellington Avenue West, Rideau Avenue West and Queen Street West.
- About 190 buildings fill the blocks, and they get taller toward Downtown: glass towers up to about 230 m, condo towers, mid-rise offices and heritage low-rises. They are drawn as merged meshes so the frame rate holds.
- Traffic lights stand at about 40 junctions, and the traffic stops on red.
- There is more traffic and many more pedestrians.
- Police cruisers drive in the traffic, with flashing light bars.
- **Rideau Avenue West** has eight businesses with storefronts. Press E at the door:

  | Business | What you can do |

  | Maple Travel | Buy a $20 express ticket to any station |

  | River Bakery | Buy food |

**Ridge Centre Mall**

- You can walk in through the glass doors on the parking side, or through the back door.
- Inside:
  - two anchor stores: Market Hall and Home + Garden;
  - eight shops: books, gadgets, clothing, sports, noodles, pizza, toys and security;
  - a food court, planters and benches;
  - the info desk.
- The info desk gives side jobs:
  - the lost teddy bear;
  - a mall walker survey;
  - a food court run.
- The Job Centre's courier job also ends at the info desk.
- The active side job shows in the HUD with the distance to its next stop.

**Interiors**

- Riverbank Financial and Northshore Residences now have furnished lobbies: a reception desk, a seating area, lift doors, plants and art.
- Their 16th-floor suites are furnished too: a client suite, and a show condo with a living room, kitchen, dining table and bedroom.
- The Caivan office gains a lounge, plants, art and a bookshelf.

**University of Vataloo**

The campus is laid out north to south like the University of Waterloo's campus map:

- Columbia Street runs along the north edge, and Ring Road loops inside it.
- **North:** the PAC and SLC, EXP, BMH, M3 and the Davis Centre.
- **Middle:** QNC, MC and M4, with C2 and ESC to the east.
- **South-middle:** Needles Hall, STC and B1, then the Dana Porter Library with ML and Tatham beside it.
- **South:** EV3, EV1, AL, Hagey Hall and PAS.
- **Outside the ring:** Engineering 7 and 5 with their skybridge, and St. Jerome's across Laurel Creek.
- A grid of footpaths connects every door, and the campus has lawns and a rock garden.

**Vehicles**

- **Engine sound:** every vehicle has its own engine: four-cylinder, turbo four, straight six, V12, V8, V6, diesel, the electric motor, the outboard, the jet ski, the helicopter turbine and the plane's propeller. The revs follow speed, gears and throttle, and the tyres squeal in a drift.
- **Volume:** use the slider under the map. V mutes and unmutes. Your setting is remembered.
- **Drifting:** it depends on the car. Light rear-drive cars (Sterling 88, Breeze RS, Volt GT) hold a slide under power after a tap of the handbrake (Space). The Apex V12 grips harder. Trucks, the SUV and the bus barely slide.
- **Airport helicopter:** there is now a helicopter on a helipad by the control tower, free to fly.
- **Planes:**
  - Hold W to take off: the plane lifts off at 22 m/s and climbs out for a few seconds.
  - Space lifts it off earlier.
  - On the ground, S slows the plane, then reverses it, and it steers like a car in reverse.
