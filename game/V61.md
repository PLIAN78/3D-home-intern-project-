# V6.1 - University of Vataloo and terrain corrections

- The city and northern mainland now stand at ground level. Southern woodland has gentle hills under 7 m, with continuous road grading.
- Heron Gardens and Elias's island are one island, completely surrounded by navigable water. The causeway is removed.
- Three harbours each provide one motorboat and one passenger motor ship. Walk onto a pier and press F; use W/S and A/D. Slow down beside a pier or dry shoreline before F to leave.
- Express stations now have large signed ground entrances, doors, a visible lift shaft, canopy and departure access with E.
- University of Vataloo uses a Waterloo-inspired Ring Road and pedestrian network, with procedural interpretations of Dana Porter, Davis, MC, QNC, Engineering 5/7, SLC/PAC, Needles, Tatham, Hagey and Environment 3. Principal Chen is at Needles Hall; subject lessons and co-op recruitment remain playable.
- The airport adds two terminals, gates and jet bridges, hangars, control tower, parking and a pedestrian bridge. Two training aircraft can taxi, take off and land. F boards/exits; W/S changes throttle, A/D steers/banks, Space takes off/climbs, Shift descends. Exit only after landing and stopping.
- Southwest woodland uses seeded groves and clearings rather than sparse random tree dots.
