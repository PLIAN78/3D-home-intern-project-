# V6.4: the rebuilt campus, Heron Fields, Heron Bay, swimming and a paved north shore

**Fixes**

- **Heron Bridge, north end.** The road stub from the bridge stopped 4 m short of Queen Street West, leaving a strip of grass between the two roads. Both north bridgeheads now run all the way to the riverfront street.
- **Helipad.** The pad sat at exactly the same height as the airport apron, so their surfaces flickered into each other at the pad's edges. The pad now stands just above the apron. The taxiway crossings at the ends of the runway had the same problem and are fixed too.
- **River banks.** The bank strips on the north side of the river were drawn upside down and showed as a black band. Every strip now faces up.
- **Windows build.** `world/shores.ts` and `world/Shores.tsx` differed only by letter case, which broke the build on Windows. The data file is now `world/shoreline.ts`, and a test stops two files from ever differing only by case again.

**University of Vataloo**

The campus has been rebuilt from the University of Waterloo campus map (north up, at about half scale):

- **Ring Road** follows the map's loop. Hagey Boulevard and a short north link join it to Columbia Street. **University Avenue** runs down the east side and meets the ring.
- **Columbia Street** now carries straight on from the South Commons avenue past University Station. The two roads used to stop short of each other by the station.
- **44 buildings** stand where they are on the map, turned at the map's angle (most at 28°):
  - **North-west:** AHS, BMH, LHI, PAC, UC and SLC.
  - **North:** ERC, CSB, COM, GSC and M3.
  - **Middle:** MC, DC, C2, ESC, EIT and E3.
  - **South-middle:** QNC, B1, B2, STC, NH, PHY, E2, CPH, RCH, DWE and GH.
  - **South:** the Dana Porter Library, ML, AL, TC, SCH, EV1–3, Hagey Hall and PAS.
  - **Outside the ring:** E5, E6, E7, ECH, EC2 and EC4.

  Each building has its own look, a door on the side its path leaves from, a canopy, and its name and code over the door. Several, such as Needles Hall, have more than one wing.
- **Skybridges** link the buildings that are joined on the map, for example PAC–SLC, MC–QNC, DC–C2, E5–E3 and E7–E6.
- **Paths.**
  - Main walks cross the campus, including the "Frosty Line" east–west through the south-middle.
  - A path runs from every door to a walk or to a Ring Road sidewalk.
  - A paved plaza sits between PAC, SLC and MC.
- **Laurel Creek** runs west of the ring between two ponds.
- **The map** labels every building with its code.
- **Lessons, the principal and co-ops** are where they were:
  - Principal Chen waits outside Needles Hall.
  - Lessons are at E7, STC, TC and Hagey Hall.
  - The café is in SLC and the gym is in PAC.
  - Press E at each building's door.

**Heron Fields**

- The empty land between Lake Road and Quarry Lane is a new neighbourhood.
- Fieldstone Avenue, Clover Crescent and Meadow Way carry on west from North Meadows, crossed by Heron Fields Drive, Wren Lane and Bluebell Lane.
- 150 new lots hold detached homes and freehold towns. About a quarter are still under construction.
- The "north woods" blueprint is now on the Heron Fields green.

**Heron Bay and swimming**

- The land south-west of Heron Lake, between the lake, the river and the airport, is now open water: **Heron Bay**. It is west of the boat dock and Heron Lake Station, and both stay on dry land. The bay has a shore strip like the other shores.
- **You can swim.** In deep water (the lake, the bay or the river) you swim instead of walking:
  - You lie flat and do the front crawl, or tread water when you stop.
  - You swim at 2.4 m/s, or 3.8 m/s with Shift.
  - You can't jump.
  - You climb out where the bottom rises.

**The north shore is paved**

- Everything north of the river is grey paving now, with joints every 3 m, right down to the water and over the bridge abutments.
- The river's north bank is a stone edge.
- Heron Gardens Island keeps its lawns. Owawa Square keeps its green.
- The map shows the north shore in grey.
